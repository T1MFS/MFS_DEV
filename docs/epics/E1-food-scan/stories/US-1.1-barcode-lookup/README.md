# US-1.1 Look up nutrition by barcode

[← E1 Food scan and nutrition](../../README.md)

**As a** user, **I want** to scan a product barcode **so that** I see its nutrition without typing.

**Acceptance:** Scan captures a photo, detects a barcode with Apple Vision, and shows one-serving and whole-package nutrition with an Open Food Facts attribution link.
