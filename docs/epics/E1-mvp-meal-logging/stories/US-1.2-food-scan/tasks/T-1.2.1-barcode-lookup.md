# T-1.2.1 Look up nutrition by barcode

[← US-1.2](../README.md) · [Epic E1](../../../README.md)

**As a** user, **I want** to scan a product barcode **so that** I see its nutrition without typing.

**Acceptance:** Scan captures a photo, detects a barcode with Apple Vision, and shows one-serving and whole-package nutrition with an Open Food Facts attribution link.

## Subtasks

### ST-1.2.1.1 Capture photo and detect barcode

**Status:** Done

- **Input:** camera photo captured when Scan is tapped.
- **Output:** barcode string, or a "no barcode" error.
- **Where:** `NutritionBarcodeScanner` in `Sources/NutritionScanning.swift` (Apple Vision).
- **Test:** fixture-based scan test using `Tests/MFSTests/Fixtures/IMG_0270.HEIC`.


### ST-1.2.1.2 Open Food Facts v3 client

**Status:** Done

- **Input:** barcode string.
- **Output:** `OpenFoodFactsProduct` with unrounded per-100 g/ml values, or `OpenFoodFactsError`.
- **Where:** `OpenFoodFactsClient` in `Sources/NutritionScanning.swift`.
- **Test:** stubbed Open Food Facts response in `NutritionScanCaptureTests.swift`.
