# T-1.4.1 Build a complete food record

[← US-1.4](../README.md) · [Epic E1](../../../README.md)

**As a** user, **I want** my accepted food turned into a full Tidepool-standard food record **so that** it can be sent to DigitalMe.

**Acceptance:** The record includes brand, code, meal, name and event context, passes field validation, and never invents a consumption time from a scan.

## Subtasks

### ST-1.4.1.1 Collect and validate missing event fields

**Status:** Planned

- **Needs:** meal (optional), consumption time, brand (optional in source), name; strings 1–100 characters.
- **Rule:** a scan is not evidence of consumption; data is passed to the manage tab. Once entered on the manage tab it is send as a record to digitalme. 
- **Output:** validated event context, or a list of field errors.
- **Reference:** Tidepool Food schema (see the [project README](../../../../../../README.md)).


### ST-1.4.1.2 Food record builder

**Status:** Planned

- **Input:** `TidepoolPreparedFood` plus validated event context ([ST-1.4.1.1](#st-1411-collect-and-validate-missing-event-fields)).
- **Output:** complete Tidepool `food` record as Encodable.
- **Rule:** do not hide unsupported nutrients in arbitrary payload fields; do not reuse insulin or bolus-calculator events.
- **Tests:** JSON keys, units, length limits, missing-field rejection.
