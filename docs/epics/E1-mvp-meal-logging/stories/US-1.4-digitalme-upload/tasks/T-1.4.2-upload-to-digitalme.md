# T-1.4.2 Upload to DigitalMe

[← US-1.4](../README.md) · [Epic E1](../../../README.md)

**As a** user, **I want** to send a confirmed food record to DigitalMe **so that** it appears with my other data.

**Acceptance:** Explicit user confirmation before upload; failures are reported and retryable; nothing uploads automatically.

## Subtasks

### ST-1.4.2.1 Decide DigitalMe transport and authentication

**Status:** Open question

- **Known:** DigitalMe uses the Tidepool data standard; its transport is not necessarily Tidepool's.
- **Decision needed:** endpoint, auth method, and whether TidepoolKit is useful. It is currently skipped.
- **Output:** a short decision note added here.


### ST-1.4.2.2 Upload client with retry

**Status:** Planned. Blocked by [ST-1.4.2.1](#st-1421-decide-digitalme-transport-and-authentication).

- **Input:** complete food record from [ST-1.4.1.2](T-1.4.1-complete-food-record.md#st-1412-food-record-builder), explicit user confirmation.
- **Output:** success, or a retryable error shown to the user.
- **Rule:** never upload automatically; store credentials in the Keychain.
